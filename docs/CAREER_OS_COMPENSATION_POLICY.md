# Career OS Compensation Policy

- Canonical minimum compensation floor: **$200,000 USD**.
- Effective date: 2026-07-28.
- Supersedes prior $225,000 and $250,000 thresholds.
- Do not reject a role solely because compensation is not published.
- A published range reaching or exceeding $200,000 passes the compensation screen, subject to role fit and location policy.
- Clearly below-floor roles remain held or rejected unless a documented exception applies.
